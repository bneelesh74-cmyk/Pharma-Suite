How the customer incentive works

Incentive is a reward for on-time payment, on tiered slabs. Slab rates are editable in Settings.

On-time collection slabIncentive rate
0 – ₹ 1 L0.5%
₹ 1 L – ₹ 3 L1%
Above ₹ 3 L1.5%

Example — a customer who pays ₹ 3.6 L on time earns: first ₹ 1 L at 0.5% = ₹ 500 · next ₹ 2 L at 1% = ₹ 2,000 · remaining ₹ 0.6 L at 1.5% = ₹ 900 → ₹ 3,400. Late payments do not count toward the incentive.

Dates come from the ERP ERP. Reminders appear 30 days before the due date. Clicking Send opens the compose panel where the channel and message are chosen.

How it is calculated

A representative with ₹ 18.2 L sales earns: first ₹ 1 L at 1% = ₹ 1,000 · next ₹ 4 L at 1.5% = ₹ 6,000 · next ₹ 5 L at 2% = ₹ 10,000 · remaining ₹ 8.2 L at 2.5% = ₹ 20,500 → ₹ 37,500 total. Each portion is paid at its own slab rate.
